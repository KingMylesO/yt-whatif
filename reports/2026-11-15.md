## Decisions and notes
- **Length:** the voice ran faster than the runbook's 0.42 s/word. First voice pass had rings 7:56 and photo 7:49 (below 8:00). I added sourced facts (no padding) and re-voiced: final 8:15 / 8:12 / 8:11 at ~1,290–1,300 words. Treat ~0.38 s/word as the real rate; aim for ≥1,300 words.
