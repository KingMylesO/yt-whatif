# Ideas — 2026-11-23 (batch 06)

15 what-ifs mixing space, Earth and the human body. None repeat `topics.md`. Ideas 1–10 are in production this batch; 11–15 are for future runs. **Happening now** (CLAUDE.md step 2): ideas 1–10 and 11–12 hang off real events from 2–23 November 2026 or coming up within days.

## In production (batch 06)

### 1. What If You Lived on Mercury? (space, long-form `b6_mercury`) — HAPPENING NOW
- **Hook:** On 21 November 2026, ESA and JAXA's BepiColombo went into orbit around Mercury, only the second mission ever to do it. What if you went with it and tried to live there?
- **Why it works:** Fresh news plus a planet full of "wait, really?" facts: a day that lasts two years, a double sunrise, 430 °C afternoons and ice at the poles.
- **Confirmed / unknown:** capture confirmed by ESA flight dynamics (22 Nov, 00:56 UTC); the two orbiters separate 9–10 Dec; science starts April 2027.
- **Sources:** [ESA: BepiColombo arrival updates](https://www.esa.int/Science_Exploration/Space_Science/BepiColombo/Latest_updates_BepiColombo_s_arrival_at_Mercury) · [EarthSky, 22 Nov 2026: BepiColombo went into orbit around Mercury](https://earthsky.org/space/bepicolombo-mission-mercury/) · [NASA: Mercury facts](https://science.nasa.gov/mercury/facts)

### 2. We Just Reached Mercury. Could You Live There? (teaser Short `short_b6_mercury`) — HAPPENING NOW
- **Hook:** A spacecraft just reached Mercury. Could you survive there?
- **Why it works:** The long-form's strongest beats in 40 seconds, dated to the arrival.
- **Sources:** as idea 1.

### 3. On Mercury, One Day Lasts Two Years (standalone Short `short_b6_mday`)
- **Hook:** Mercury's year is 88 Earth days, but sunrise to sunrise takes about 176.
- **Why it works:** A counterintuitive number with a clean explanation (the 3:2 spin–orbit resonance) and a visual payoff (the double sunrise).
- **Sources:** [NASA: Mercury facts](https://science.nasa.gov/mercury/facts) · [ESA: BepiColombo mission](https://www.esa.int/Science_Exploration/Space_Science/BepiColombo)

### 4. What If You Texted Voyager 1 Right Now? (extra Short `short_b6_voyager`) — HAPPENING NOW
- **Hook:** On 18 November 2026, Voyager 1 became the first spacecraft one light-day from Earth. A message now takes a full day each way.
- **Why it works:** A once-ever milestone, easy to picture, and a calm note that it's been sending no data since 14 Nov.
- **Sources:** [Spaceflight Now, 18 Nov 2026](https://spaceflightnow.com/2026/11/18/nasas-voyager-1-is-one-light-day-from-earth/) · [NASA JPL: Voyager mission status](https://science.nasa.gov/mission/voyager/)

### 5. What If You Lived in Space for 10 Years? (body, long-form `b6_orbit`) — HAPPENING NOW + owner's backlog ("10 years in space")
- **Hook:** One of the Shenzhou-23 astronauts is set to spend about a year on China's Tiangong station, its first year-long mission. What would ten years do to your body?
- **Why it works:** Ties the backlog topic to the news, with hard data (NASA Twins Study, Polyakov's 437 days, Kononenko's 1,111 days) and an honest "nobody knows" for the ten-year extrapolation.
- **Confirmed / unknown:** which astronaut stays a year hasn't been announced; Shenzhou-24 (first foreign visitor, from Pakistan) hadn't launched as of 23 Nov.
- **Sources:** [Space.com, 12 Nov 2026: Pakistani astronaut to visit Tiangong](https://www.space.com/space-exploration/human-spaceflight/pakistani-astronaut-set-to-become-1st-international-visitor-to-chinas-tiangong-space-station-this-month) · [Garrett-Bakelman et al. 2019, Science: the NASA Twins Study](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.aau8650) · [NASA: human research, bone and muscle loss](https://www.nasa.gov/hrp/)

### 7. Why Astronauts Come Home Taller (standalone Short `short_b6_taller`)
- **Hook:** In orbit your spine stretches and you can grow up to about 3% taller. It doesn't last.
- **Why it works:** A surprising, true body fact with a twist (back pain, higher disc-injury risk after landing).
- **Sources:** see `scripts/_b6_orbit_claims.md` (NASA; Chang et al. 2016, Spine).

### 8. What If the Ozone Layer Disappeared? (extra Short `short_b6_ozone`) — HAPPENING NOW
- **Hook:** On 4 November 2026, NASA and NOAA said this year's Antarctic ozone hole averaged about 25 million km², the largest in a decade.
- **Why it works:** Real news plus a what-if with a hopeful twist (the Montreal Protocol; recovery by the 2070s).
- **Sources:** [NASA: 2026 ozone hole](https://science.nasa.gov/earth/2026-ozone-hole/) · [NOAA Ozone Watch](https://ozonewatch.gsfc.nasa.gov/)

### 9. We Hit an Asteroid. Now We're Checking the Damage (extra Short `short_b6_dimorphos`) — HAPPENING NOW
- **Hook:** This month ESA's Hera arrived at Dimorphos, the asteroid NASA's DART hit on purpose in 2022.
- **Why it works:** Planetary defence, live; first close-ups are due 25 Nov.
- **Sources:** [ESA, 11 Nov 2026: Hera in "rectangular orbit"](https://www.esa.int/articles/hera-spacecraft-in-rectangular-orbit-around-target-asteroids) · [Johns Hopkins APL: DART altered the asteroid's orbit](https://hub.jhu.edu/2022/10/11/dart-successfully-altered-asteroid-orbit/)

## For future runs

### 11. What If Antarctica's Ice Cliffs Kept Collapsing? (Earth) — HAPPENING NOW
- **Hook:** A study in *Science* this month tracked 15,000 Antarctic ice cliffs from space. They collapse faster than models predicted, then stop.
- **Why it works:** A feared "runaway" turns out to have brakes (mélange, ridges, thinning), but the ice sheet isn't safe.
- **Sources:** [Needell, Walker & Bassis 2026, Science](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.aee0658) · [ScienceDaily: Antarctica's ice cliffs are collapsing faster than expected](https://www.sciencedaily.com/releases/2026/11/261117033925.htm)

### 12. What If a Meteor Storm Hit Tonight? (space) — HAPPENING NOW
- **Hook:** The Leonids peaked on 17–18 November. In 1833 and 1966 they rained thousands of meteors an hour.
- **Why it works:** Seasonal, visual, with real history.
- **Sources:** [NASA: Leonids](https://science.nasa.gov/solar-system/meteors-meteorites/leonids) · [Al Jazeera, 17 Nov 2026: Leonid meteor shower 2026](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/11/17/leonid-meteor-shower-2026-when-and-where-to-see-it-from-where-you-live)

### 13. What If Earth Stopped Spinning? (space/Earth, owner's backlog)
- **Hook:** Earth's surface moves at about 1,670 km/h at the equator. Stop it, and everything not bolted down keeps going.
- **Sources:** [NASA: Earth facts](https://science.nasa.gov/earth/facts/) · [Live Science: what if Earth stopped spinning](https://www.livescience.com/planet-earth/what-would-happen-if-earth-stopped-spinning)

### 14. What If Every Volcano Erupted at Once? (Earth, owner's backlog)
- **Hook:** About 1,350 potentially active volcanoes. What if they all went off on the same day?
- **Sources:** [USGS: how many active volcanoes are there?](https://www.usgs.gov/faqs/how-many-active-volcanoes-are-there-earth) · [Smithsonian Global Volcanism Program](https://volcano.si.edu/)

### 15. What If You Only Drank Seawater? (body, owner's backlog)
- **Hook:** Seawater is about 3.5% salt; your kidneys can only make urine that's a bit less salty than that.
- **Why it works:** A survival myth with clear physiology. Explainer only, no advice.
- **Sources:** [NOAA: why can't we drink seawater?](https://oceanservice.noaa.gov/facts/drinksw.html) · [USGS: why is the ocean salty?](https://www.usgs.gov/faqs/why-ocean-salty)
