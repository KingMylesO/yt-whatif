# Ideas — 2026-11-20 (batch 05)

25 what-ifs mixing space, Earth and the human body. None repeat `topics.md`. Ideas 1–11 are in production this batch (backlog items used first); 12–25 are for future runs.

## In production (batch 05)

### 1. What If the Moon Disappeared? (space, long-form `b5_moon`)
- **Hook:** Tonight the Moon is gone. The tides shrink to about a third of today's spring tides, corals lose their spawning cue, and eclipses end forever.
- **Why it works:** Owner's backlog. Everyone has looked at the Moon; the twist (simulations say Earth's tilt would wobble far less than the famous 1993 result feared) overturns a popular claim.
- **Sources:** [NASA NSSDC: Moon fact sheet](https://nssdc.gsfc.nasa.gov/planetary/factsheet/moonfact.html) · [NASA JPL: the Apollo experiment that keeps on giving](https://jpl.nasa.gov/news/the-apollo-experiment-that-keeps-on-giving/) · [Live Science: the Moon may have formed in 5 hours](https://www.livescience.com/space/the-moon/the-moon-may-have-formed-in-just-5-hours-new-simulations-suggest)

### 2. What If the Moon Vanished Tonight? (teaser Short `short_b5_moon`)
- **Hook:** No Moon tonight: darker nights, weaker tides, confused corals.
- **Why it works:** The long-form's strongest beats in 35 seconds.
- **Sources:** as idea 1.

### 3. The Moon Is Slowly Leaving Us (standalone Short `short_b5_drift`)
- **Hook:** Lasers bounced off mirrors left by Apollo astronauts show the Moon drifting 3.8 cm a year.
- **Why it works:** A real, measurable "wait, really?" with a far-future payoff: no more total eclipses.
- **Sources:** [NASA JPL: Apollo laser ranging](https://jpl.nasa.gov/news/the-apollo-experiment-that-keeps-on-giving/) · [Live Science: will Earth ever lose its moon?](https://www.livescience.com/space/the-moon/will-earth-ever-lose-its-moon)

### 4. What If Pluto Replaced Our Moon? (extra standalone Short `short_b5_pluto`)
- **Hook:** Swap the Moon for Pluto: a smaller disc that could still outshine today's full Moon.
- **Why it works:** Owner's backlog; a fun size-and-brightness comparison with real NASA numbers.
- **Sources:** [NASA: Pluto facts](https://science.nasa.gov/dwarf-planets/pluto/facts) · [NASA NSSDC: Pluto fact sheet](https://nssdc.gsfc.nasa.gov/planetary/factsheet/plutofact.html)

### 5. What If All the Ice on Earth Melted? (Earth, long-form `b5_ice`)
- **Hook:** Melt every glacier and ice sheet and the seas rise about 70 m — yet next to Greenland the sea would fall.
- **Why it works:** Owner's backlog. Maps, cities and a counterintuitive gravity twist.
- **Sources:** [USGS: sea level if all glaciers melted](https://www.usgs.gov/faqs/how-would-sea-level-change-if-glaciers-melted) · [NSIDC: ice sheet quick facts](https://nsidc.org/learn/parts-cryosphere/ice-sheets/ice-sheet-quick-facts) · [Australian Antarctic Program: the ice sheet](https://www.antarctica.gov.au/about-antarctica/ice-and-atmosphere/ice-sheet/)

### 6. What If All the Ice on Earth Melted? (teaser Short `short_b5_ice`)
- **Hook:** Antarctica alone holds 58 m of sea level.
- **Why it works:** Cut-down of the long-form with the gravity twist as the payoff.
- **Sources:** as idea 5.

### 7. A Lake Hidden Under 4 km of Antarctic Ice (standalone Short `short_b5_vostok`)
- **Hook:** A lake the size of Lake Ontario, sealed under nearly 4 km of ice for millions of years.
- **Why it works:** Mystery plus "could anything live there?".
- **Sources:** [Live Science: Russian team breaches Lake Vostok](https://www.livescience.com/18369-success-russian-team-breaches-buried-antarctic-lake-vostok.html) · [Science News Explores: Antarctica, land of lakes](https://www.snexplores.org/article/explainer-antarctica-land-lakes)

### 8. It Rained for 2 Million Years (extra standalone Short `short_b5_rain`)
- **Hook:** About 234 million years ago Earth turned wet, on and off, for up to 2 million years, and dinosaurs may have taken over because of it.
- **Why it works:** Sounds made up; it's the Carnian Pluvial Episode.
- **Sources:** [Live Science: the Triassic's million-year rainstorm](https://www.livescience.com/carnian-pluvial-episode-mass-extinction.html) · [University of Bristol: dinosaurs originated with a bang (2018)](https://www.bristol.ac.uk/news/2018/april/dinosaurs-ended-and-originated-with-a-bang-.html)

### 9. What If All Your Bacteria Suddenly Vanished? (body, long-form `b5_microbes`)
- **Hook:** 38 trillion bacteria live in and on you. At midnight, every one disappears.
- **Why it works:** Owner's backlog. Germ-free animals make it testable, and the twist is you can't stay sterile.
- **Sources:** [Sender, Fuchs & Milo 2016, PLOS Biology](https://journals.plos.org/plosbiology/article?id=10.1371/journal.pbio.1002533) · [Bionumbers: mass of bacteria in the body](https://bionumbers.hms.harvard.edu/bionumber.aspx?id=113003)

### 10. All Your Bacteria Vanish Overnight (teaser Short `short_b5_microbes`)
- **Hook:** Lose 200 g of bacteria and your colon cells run low on fuel.
- **Sources:** as idea 9.

### 11. You're About Half Bacteria (standalone Short `short_b5_cells`)
- **Hook:** The famous "10 bacteria for every human cell" came from a 1972 back-of-the-envelope estimate.
- **Why it works:** Myth-busting with a funny toilet-trip punchline.
- **Sources:** [Sender et al. 2016](https://journals.plos.org/plosbiology/article?id=10.1371/journal.pbio.1002533) · [Science News: bacteria don't outnumber human cells so much after all](https://www.sciencenews.org/article/bodys-bacteria-dont-outnumber-human-cells-so-much-after-all)

## For future runs

### 12. What If Earth Stopped Spinning? (space/Earth, backlog)
- **Hook:** At the equator you're moving at about 1,670 km/h. Stop the spin and everything not bolted to the bedrock keeps going.
- **Why it works:** Classic what-if with a slower-stop variant (oceans migrate to the poles) as the twist.
- **Sources:** [Space.com: what if Earth stopped spinning](https://www.space.com/what-if-earth-stopped-spinning.html) · [BBC Sky at Night: what would happen if Earth stopped rotating](https://skyatnightmagazine.com/space-science/what-happen-earth-stopped-rotating) · [Universe Today](https://www.universetoday.com/26623/what-would-happen-if-the-earth-stopped-spinning/)

### 13. What If You Fell Into Jupiter? (space, backlog)
- **Hook:** Jupiter has no surface to land on. You just keep falling, into hotter, denser gas, until it behaves like metal.
- **Why it works:** A descent story with Juno's real findings at each layer.
- **Sources:** [SwRI Juno: inside the giant](https://www.missionjuno.swri.edu/jupiter/the-interior/) · [Space.com: how can Jupiter have no surface?](https://www.space.com/the-universe/jupiter/how-can-jupiter-have-no-surface-a-dive-into-a-planet-so-big-it-could-swallow-1-000-earths)

### 14. What If Earth Orbited Two Suns? (space, backlog)
- **Hook:** Kepler-16b really has two sunsets a day.
- **Why it works:** Real exoplanets make it more than sci-fi; double shadows and odd seasons.
- **Sources:** [Live Science: Kepler-16b orbits two suns](https://www.livescience.com/16095-tatooine-planet-2-suns-star-wars-kepler-16b.html) · [Scientific American: the discovery of planets with two suns](https://www.scientificamerican.com/article/the-discovery-of-planets-with-two-suns/)

### 15. What If a Day Lasted Longer Than a Year? (space, backlog)
- **Hook:** On Venus one spin takes 243 Earth days, longer than its 225-day year.
- **Why it works:** Mind-bending calendar maths, plus Venus spins backwards.
- **Sources:** [Snopes: one day on Venus is longer than a year](https://www.snopes.com/fact-check/one-day-on-venus-is-longer-than-a-year-on-venus/) · [Universe Today: rotation of Venus](https://www.universetoday.com/articles/rotation-of-venus)

### 16. What If the Solar System Drifted Into a Cold Cloud? (space, backlog "Earth inside a nebula")
- **Hook:** About 2 million years ago, one study says, a dense interstellar cloud may have squeezed the Sun's protective bubble inside Earth's orbit.
- **Why it works:** A real hypothesis with a link to Earth's climate; perfect for "this may have happened".
- **Sources:** [Boston University (Opher et al. 2024)](https://www.bu.edu/articles/2024/the-solar-system-may-have-passed-through-interstellar-clouds/) · [ScienceDaily](https://sciencedaily.com/releases/2024/06/240610140249.htm)

### 17. What If a Star Passed Through the Solar System? (space)
- **Hook:** Simulations say a passing star has a small chance of flinging planets around before the Sun dies.
- **Why it works:** Turns the backlog's "rogue planet" into a sourced study.
- **Sources:** [ScienceAlert: ways a rogue star could doom the solar system](https://www.sciencealert.com/new-study-lists-the-ways-a-rogue-star-could-spell-doom-for-our-solar-system) · [Gizmodo: a rogue star could hurl Earth into deep space](https://gizmodo.com/a-rogue-star-could-hurl-earth-into-deep-space-study-warns-2000608784)

### 18. What If Every Volcano Erupted at Once? (Earth, backlog)
- **Hook:** About 1,350 volcanoes on Earth could erupt again. Now they all go at once.
- **Why it works:** Huge map visual; real cooling examples (Tambora's "year without a summer").
- **Sources:** [USGS: how many active volcanoes are there?](https://usgs.gov/faqs/how-many-active-volcanoes-are-there-earth) · [Smithsonian Global Volcanism Program](https://volcano.si.edu/)

### 19. What If the Oceans Were Twice as Deep? (Earth, backlog)
- **Hook:** The average ocean is about 3.7 km deep. Double it, and where does the water go?
- **Why it works:** Simple premise, surprising answer: most of today's land would drown.
- **Sources:** [NOAA: how deep is the ocean?](https://oceanservice.noaa.gov/facts/oceandepth.html) · [NOAA Ocean Exploration: ocean depth](https://Oceanexplorer.noaa.gov/facts/ocean-depth.html)

### 20. What If Every Ant Disappeared? (Earth, backlog)
- **Hook:** There are about 20 quadrillion ants, 2.5 million for every person.
- **Why it works:** A tiny animal with a planet-sized job (soil, seeds, pest control).
- **Sources:** [Schultheiss et al. 2022, PNAS](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9546634) · [Phys.org: 20 quadrillion ants](https://phys.org/news/2022-09-ants-earth-quadrillion.html)

### 21. What If Days Were Still 19 Hours Long? (Earth, backlog "fast-spinning early Earth")
- **Hook:** For about a billion years, Earth's day may have been stuck at 19 hours.
- **Why it works:** A real 2023 result about a tidal "stall" — and a natural follow-up to the Moon video.
- **Sources:** [Mitchell & Kirscher 2023, Nature Geoscience](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41561-023-01202-6.pdf) · [ScienceAlert: a day used to last 19 hours](https://www.sciencealert.com/a-day-on-earth-used-to-last-only-19-hours-now-we-know-why)

### 22. What If You Only Drank Seawater? (body, backlog)
- **Hook:** Seawater is saltier than your kidneys can handle, so drinking it makes you thirstier.
- **Why it works:** Clear body-chemistry explainer (no advice), with sea birds' salt glands as the twist.
- **Sources:** [NOAA: can humans drink seawater?](https://oceanservice.noaa.gov/facts/drinksw.html) · [USGS: why can't people drink seawater?](https://www.usgs.gov/water-science-school/science/water-qa-why-cant-people-drink-seawater) · [Live Science: why can't we drink saltwater?](https://www.livescience.com/32454-why-cant-we-drink-saltwater.html)

### 23. What If You Spent 10 Years in Space? (body, backlog)
- **Hook:** NASA's one-year twin experiment found most changes reversed back on Earth, but not all.
- **Why it works:** Real data, extrapolated carefully (bone, eyes, radiation). Avoid likenesses; text-only mention of the study.
- **Sources:** [PBS NewsHour: NASA year-in-space twin study](https://www.pbs.org/newshour/amp/science/nasa-year-in-space-twin-study) · [CNN: NASA twins study results](https://amp.cnn.com/cnn/2019/04/11/health/nasa-kelly-twins-study-results)

### 24. What If You Had an Octopus's Blue Blood? (body, backlog)
- **Hook:** Octopus blood carries oxygen with copper, not iron, and turns blue.
- **Why it works:** Colour-changing visual; real Antarctic-octopus research on why it helps in the cold.
- **Sources:** [Scientific American: octopuses survive sub-zero temps with blue blood](https://www.scientificamerican.com/blog/octopus-chronicles/octopuses-survive-sub-zero-temps-thanks-to-specialized-blue-blood/) · [Live Science: Antarctic octopus's blue blood](https://www.livescience.com/50322-antarctic-octopus-blue-blood.html)

### 25. What If You Breathed Like a Bird? (body, backlog)
- **Hook:** Birds' air flows one way through their lungs, which is how some geese cross the Himalayas.
- **Why it works:** A body "upgrade" with a real-world champion.
- **Sources:** [Carnegie Museum: how birds breathe](https://carnegiemnh.org/how-birds-breathe-with-their-butts) · [Gigazine: why birds can fly at 8,000 m](https://gigazine.net/gsc_news/en/20211017-why-bird-fly-over-mount-everest)
