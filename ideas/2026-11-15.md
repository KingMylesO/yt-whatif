# Ideas — 2026-11-15 (batch 03)

## 1. What If Earth Had Rings Like Saturn? (in production, batch 03)
- **Category:** Space
- **Hook:** Earth may really have had a ring 466 million years ago: 21 craters from that time all sit within 30° of the old equator.
- **Why it works:** It's a familiar sci-fi image backed by a 2024 discovery, and the sky visuals are easy wins (ring arcs, ring shadows, seasons).
- **Sources:** [Monash University: Earth may have had a ring system 466 million years ago](https://www.monash.edu/science/news-events/news/2024/earth-may-have-had-a-ring-system-466-million-years-ago) · [Space.com: Earth had Saturn-like rings 466 million years ago](https://space.com/ancient-earth-ring-system-asteroid-breakup)

## 2. What If Saturn Lost Its Rings? (in production, batch 03)
- **Category:** Space
- **Hook:** Saturn's rings are raining into the planet. Enough ice to fill an Olympic pool falls every half hour, and the rings could be gone in under 100 million years.
- **Why it works:** Most viewers assume the rings are permanent, so learning that we live in a short "ringed" window is a strong twist.
- **Sources:** [NASA: Saturn is losing its rings at "worst-case-scenario" rate](https://science.nasa.gov/solar-system/nasa-research-reveals-saturn-is-losing-its-rings-at-worst-case-scenario-rate) · [NASA JPL: Cassini data show Saturn's rings relatively new (10–100 million years)](https://www.jpl.nasa.gov/news/nasas-cassini-data-show-saturns-rings-relatively-new)

## 3. What If Earth's Magnetic Field Switched Off? (in production, batch 03)
- **Category:** Earth
- **Hook:** Mars lost its global magnetic field, and NASA's MAVEN mission caught the solar wind stripping its atmosphere into space.
- **Why it works:** An invisible shield we never think about, with a ready-made cautionary tale next door. It also shows auroras, satellites and the power grid at risk.
- **Sources:** [NASA SVS: Solar wind strips the Martian atmosphere (MAVEN)](https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/4370/) · [NASA: Earth's magnetosphere, protecting our planet from harmful space energy](https://science.nasa.gov/science-research/earth-science/earths-magnetosphere-protecting-our-planet-from-harmful-space-energy/)

## 4. What If Earth's Magnetic Poles Flipped Tomorrow? (in production, batch 03)
- **Category:** Earth
- **Hook:** Earth's poles have swapped 183 times in the last 83 million years. The last full flip was about 780,000 years ago, and 41,000 years ago the field briefly fell to 5% of today's strength.
- **Why it works:** It's a real, recurring event with a "we're overdue?" hook, and compasses pointing south is an image everyone gets.
- **Sources:** [NASA: Flip Flop, why variations in Earth's magnetic field aren't causing today's climate change](https://science.nasa.gov/science-research/earth-science/flip-flop-why-variations-in-earths-magnetic-field-arent-causing-todays-climate-change) · [ESA: Sound of Earth's magnetic flip 41,000 years ago (Laschamp event)](https://www.esa.int/ESA_Multimedia/Videos/2024/10/Sound_of_Earth_s_magnetic_flip_41_000_years_ago) · [NOAA NCEI: Wandering of the geomagnetic poles (~55 km per year)](https://www.ncei.noaa.gov/products/wandering-geomagnetic-poles)

## 5. What If Humans Could Photosynthesize? (in production, batch 03)
- **Category:** Body
- **Hook:** One vertebrate already half-does it: spotted salamander embryos have algae living inside their cells.
- **Why it works:** "Never eat again" is an irresistible fantasy, and the payoff math is surprising: photosynthetic skin would cover only about 1% of your daily energy, so you'd need a tennis court of skin.
- **Sources:** [National Geographic: Solar salamanders have algae in their cells](https://www.nationalgeographic.com/science/article/solar-salamanders-have-algae-in-their-cells) · [Live Science: What if humans had photosynthetic skin?](https://www.livescience.com/what-if-humans-had-green-skin-photosynthesis.html)

## 6. What If You Could Eat Sunlight Like a Sea Slug? (in production, batch 03)
- **Category:** Body
- **Hook:** The sea slug *Elysia chlorotica* steals chloroplasts from the algae it eats and can then live on photosynthesis for six to eight months.
- **Why it works:** A real animal with a superpower. A 2025 study found its relatives keep the stolen chloroplasts in "kleptosomes" as emergency food.
- **Sources:** [Rutgers: Solar-powered sea slugs shed light on search for perpetual green energy](https://www.rutgers.edu/news/solar-powered-sea-slugs-shed-light-search-perpetual-green-energy) · [Harvard Gazette: Stealing a superpower (Cell, 2025)](https://news.harvard.edu/gazette/story/2025/06/stealing-a-superpower/)

## 7. What If You Had a Mantis Shrimp's Eyes? (in production, batch 03)
- **Category:** Body
- **Hook:** Mantis shrimp have 12 kinds of colour receptors to our 3, yet in tests they're *worse* than us at telling similar colours apart.
- **Why it works:** It flips the internet myth of "they see colours we can't imagine" and lets us explain how vision actually works.
- **Sources:** [Queensland Brain Institute (UQ): Discovery gives new insight into colour vision](https://qbi.uq.edu.au/node/1874) · [National Geographic: The mantis shrimp sees like a satellite](https://www.nationalgeographic.com/science/article/the-mantis-shrimp-sees-like-a-satellite)

## 8. What If There Were No Clouds for a Week? (in production, batch 03)
- **Category:** Earth
- **Hook:** About two-thirds (67%) of Earth's surface is typically covered by clouds.
- **Why it works:** Clouds look like decoration, but taking them away changes temperature, rain and night-time cooling within days. It's a clean, visual one-week timeline.
- **Sources:** [NASA: Cloudy Earth](https://www.nasa.gov/image-article/cloudy-earth) · [NASA Earth Observatory: Changing global cloudiness (clouds' net cooling effect)](https://science.nasa.gov/earth/earth-observatory/changing-global-cloudiness/)

## 9. What If the Moon Disappeared?
- **Category:** Space
- **Hook:** The Moon is already leaving: it drifts 3.8 cm farther from Earth every year, about as fast as your fingernails grow.
- **Why it works:** It's a top-searched what-if with clear stakes (tides, nights, Earth's wobble), and the "it's already happening slowly" hook is a strong opener.
- **Sources:** [NASA: Laser beams reflected between Earth and Moon boost science](https://www.nasa.gov/missions/laser-beams-reflected-between-earth-and-moon-boost-science/) · [ESA: The Moon is drifting away from Earth](https://www.esa.int/ESA_Multimedia/Images/2021/09/Ten_things_you_didn_t_know_about_the_Moon_The_Moon_is_drifting_away_from_Earth)

## 10. What If Earth Stopped Spinning?
- **Category:** Space
- **Hook:** At the equator you're moving at about 1,670 km/h right now, and if the spin stopped, the oceans would drain toward the poles and reveal an equatorial megacontinent.
- **Why it works:** It has a violent first minute (momentum) and a strange long-term world: one day per year, two polar oceans. Both make great maps.
- **Sources:** [Esri ArcUser: What would happen if Earth stopped spinning](https://www.esri.com/about/newsroom/arcuser/what-would-happen-if-earth-stopped-spinning) · [Space.com: What would happen if Earth stopped spinning?](https://www.space.com/what-would-happen-if-earth-stopped-spinning)

## 11. What If You Fell Into Jupiter?
- **Category:** Space
- **Hook:** Jupiter has no surface to land on. Juno found that even its core is "fuzzy," partly dissolved into the metallic hydrogen around it.
- **Why it works:** It's a first-person descent with rising pressure and heat, and it never "hits bottom," which is the strongest visual idea on this list.
- **Sources:** [NASA: Jupiter facts](https://science.nasa.gov/jupiter/jupiter-facts/) · [NASA Space Place: What's it like inside Jupiter?](https://spaceplace.nasa.gov/jupiter/en/)

## 12. What If a Rogue Planet Entered Our Solar System?
- **Category:** Space
- **Hook:** There may be more planets drifting through the Milky Way with no star than there are stars, from tens of billions to trillions.
- **Why it works:** It's a real, invisible threat with a countdown structure (detection, flyby, orbital chaos), and NASA's Roman telescope is about to hunt them.
- **Sources:** [NASA: Unveiling rogue planets with NASA's Roman Space Telescope](https://www.nasa.gov/missions/roman-space-telescope/unveiling-rogue-planets-with-nasas-roman-space-telescope) · [Ohio State University: Rogue planets could outnumber the stars](https://news.osu.edu/rogue-planets-could-outnumber-the-stars)

## 13. What If a Day Lasted Longer Than a Year?
- **Category:** Space
- **Hook:** On Venus, one spin takes 243 Earth days but one orbit only 225, and the Sun rises in the west.
- **Why it works:** A real planet does it, so it's a mind-bending "this exists" fact, and putting Earth on Venus time gives a vivid scenario (months of daylight, then months of night).
- **Sources:** [NASA Space Place: All about Venus](https://spaceplace.nasa.gov/all-about-venus/en/) · [ESA: Venus and Earth compared (Venus Express)](https://sci.esa.int/web/venus-express/-/34067-venus-vs-earth)

## 14. What If Betelgeuse Exploded Tonight? (new)
- **Category:** Space
- **Hook:** When Betelgeuse goes supernova, it will shine about as bright as a half Moon and be visible in daylight for roughly a year, and it still won't harm us.
- **Why it works:** It's a real star you can find in Orion tonight, the event is guaranteed to happen eventually, and the "spectacular but safe" twist works well.
- **Sources:** [Astronomy.com: When Betelgeuse goes supernova, what will it look like from Earth?](https://www.astronomy.com/science/when-betelgeuse-goes-supernova-what-will-it-look-like-from-earth/) · [NASA: What is Betelgeuse? Inside the strange, volatile star](https://science.nasa.gov/universe/what-is-betelgeuse-inside-the-strange-volatile-star/) · [Live Science: Could a supernova ever destroy Earth?](https://www.livescience.com/space/astronomy/could-a-supernova-ever-destroy-earth)

## 15. What If All the Ice on Earth Melted?
- **Category:** Earth
- **Hook:** Melting all of Earth's land ice would raise the sea by about 70 m (230 ft), redrawing every coastline on the map.
- **Why it works:** Redrawn coastlines are made for our real-coastline map engine, and the topic is grounded in climate science without being a lecture.
- **Sources:** [USGS: How would sea level change if all glaciers melted?](https://www.usgs.gov/faqs/how-would-sea-level-change-if-glaciers-melted) · [National Geographic: Rising seas, if all the ice melted](https://www.nationalgeographic.com/magazine/article/rising-seas-ice-melt-new-shoreline-maps)

## 16. What If Every Volcano Erupted at Once?
- **Category:** Earth
- **Hook:** Earth has about 1,350 potentially active volcanoes, and just one of them, Tambora in 1815, caused the 1816 "year without a summer."
- **Why it works:** It scales a single, documented disaster up to a planetary one. The map has a striking Ring of Fire, followed by volcanic winter.
- **Sources:** [USGS: How many active volcanoes are there on Earth?](https://usgs.gov/faqs/how-many-active-volcanoes-are-there-earth) · [NASA Earth Observatory: Mount Tambora volcano](https://science.nasa.gov/earth/earth-observatory/mount-tambora-volcano-sumbawa-island-indonesia-39412/)

## 17. What If Every Ant Disappeared?
- **Category:** Earth
- **Hook:** There are about 20 quadrillion ants on Earth, and together they outweigh all wild birds and wild mammals combined.
- **Why it works:** "Tiny thing turns out to hold up the world" is a reliable twist, and soil, seeds and food webs collapse in a clear chain reaction.
- **Sources:** [Phys.org: How many ants are on Earth? 20 quadrillion, study says](https://phys.org/news/2022-09-ants-earth-quadrillion.html) · [PNAS (via PMC): The abundance, biomass, and distribution of ants on Earth](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9546634)

## 18. What If It Never Stopped Raining?
- **Category:** Earth
- **Hook:** It has happened before: about 234–232 million years ago, Earth went through a 2-million-year rainy spell, and dinosaurs came out of it on top.
- **Why it works:** It sounds like pure fantasy until the real Carnian Pluvial Episode lands, then the video can play out a modern version.
- **Sources:** [Science News: A volcano-induced rainy period made Earth's climate dinosaur-friendly](https://www.sciencenews.org/article/volcano-eruptions-rainy-period-earth-climate-dinosaur-friendly) · [Science Advances (via PMC): Extinction and dawn of the modern world in the Carnian](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7494334)

## 19. What If Earth Froze Into a Snowball Again? (new)
- **Category:** Earth
- **Hook:** 716.5 million years ago, sea ice reached all the way to the equator.
- **Why it works:** It's the opposite of the ice-melt video (a good pairing), uses a real extreme from Earth's past, and has a dramatic "how did life survive?" arc.
- **Sources:** [Harvard Gazette: Scientists find signs of "snowball Earth"](https://news.harvard.edu/gazette/story/2010/03/scientists-find-signs-of-snowball-earth) · [National Geographic: Snowball Earth, ice covered the equator 716 million years ago](https://www.nationalgeographic.com/science/article/100304-snowball-earth-ice-global-warming)

## 20. What If You Only Drank Seawater?
- **Category:** Body
- **Hook:** Your kidneys can only make urine that is less salty than seawater, so every sip leaves you more dehydrated than before.
- **Why it works:** It's a survival-myth topic with a counter-intuitive mechanism and a day-by-day body timeline, plus a payoff on how seabirds and whales cope.
- **Sources:** [NOAA Ocean Service: Can humans drink seawater?](https://oceanservice.noaa.gov/facts/drinksw.html) · [Live Science: Why can't we drink saltwater?](https://www.livescience.com/32454-why-cant-we-drink-saltwater.html)

## 21. What If You Spent 10 Years in Space?
- **Category:** Body
- **Hook:** After 340 days on the ISS, Scott Kelly's telomeres grew longer than his identical twin's, and some immune and DNA-repair genes never returned to normal.
- **Why it works:** It's a real twin experiment, and stretching it to 10 years (Mars-mission scale) makes a strong year-by-year structure.
- **Sources:** [NASA: Landmark Twins Study reveals resilience of human body in space](https://www.nasa.gov/news-release/nasas-landmark-twins-study-reveals-resilience-of-human-body-in-space/) · [NASA: Twins Study results published in Science](https://www.nasa.gov/humans-in-space/nasas-twins-study-results-published-in-science-journal/)

## 22. What If You Had Blue Blood Like an Octopus?
- **Category:** Body
- **Hook:** Octopuses have three hearts and blue, copper-based blood, and the heart that feeds the body stops beating when they swim.
- **Why it works:** Bizarre-but-true biology, an easy colour visual, and a real trade-off: hemocyanin works well in cold, low-oxygen water but is weaker for a warm-blooded runner.
- **Sources:** [Smithsonian Magazine: Ten wild facts about octopuses](https://www.smithsonianmag.com/science-nature/ten-wild-facts-about-octopuses-they-have-three-hearts-big-brains-and-blue-blood-7625828/) · [Scientific American: Octopuses survive sub-zero temps thanks to specialized blue blood](https://www.scientificamerican.com/blog/octopus-chronicles/octopuses-survive-sub-zero-temps-thanks-to-specialized-blue-blood/)

## 23. What If You Breathed With a Bird's Lungs?
- **Category:** Body
- **Hook:** Air flows through a bird's lungs in one direction only, which lets birds fly at altitudes that would knock a mammal unconscious.
- **Why it works:** It's an upgrade fantasy (climb Everest without oxygen?) with a clear mechanism to animate, plus a dinosaur link: alligators breathe this way too.
- **Sources:** [University of Utah: Gators breathe like birds](https://archive.unews.utah.edu/news_releases/gators-breathe-like-birds/) · [Scientific American: Alligators breathe like birds](https://www.scientificamerican.com/blog/observations/breathtaking-alligators-breathe-like-birds-underscoring-an-ancient-link-and-possibly-a-survival-strategy/)

## 24. What If All Your Body's Bacteria Vanished?
- **Category:** Body
- **Hook:** You carry about 38 trillion bacteria, roughly one for every one of your own 30 trillion cells.
- **Why it works:** "You're half microbe" is a jolt, and removing them turns digestion, immunity and even skin defence into a clear chain reaction.
- **Sources:** [Weizmann Institute: Germs, humans and numbers](https://wis-wander.weizmann.ac.il/node/7871) · [PLOS Biology (PubMed): Revised estimates for the number of human and bacteria cells in the body](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/27541692/)

## 25. What If You Could Regrow a Limb Like an Axolotl? (new)
- **Category:** Body
- **Hook:** An axolotl can regrow a whole leg or just a missing toe, and in 2025 scientists found it reads a chemical gradient (retinoic acid) to know which part to rebuild.
- **Why it works:** A cute animal plus a real superpower plus cutting-edge research. We have the same molecule and cells, but we scar instead of regrowing.
- **Sources:** [Northeastern University: How do axolotls regenerate their limbs?](https://news.northeastern.edu/2025/06/10/axolotl-limb-regeneration/) · [National Geographic: Axolotls can regrow limbs. Could they one day help us do the same?](https://www.nationalgeographic.com/health/article/axolotl-regrow-limbs-retinoic-acid-human-medicine)
